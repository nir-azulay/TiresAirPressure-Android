# Privacy policy

**Tires Air Pressure Management**  
Package: `com.nazulay.tiresairpressuremanagement`  
Developer: Nir Azulay, nir.azulay@gmail.com  
Last updated: 2 November 2026

This app controls your own tire-pressure controller over Bluetooth. It does not require an account, and it does not include ads or analytics.

## Information stored on your phone

The app stores this information on the phone so it can reconnect to your controller:

- The controller’s Bluetooth name and address
- The access code you enter for that controller
- The controller’s Wi-Fi network name and password, when you save them in the app
- Your pressure presets, language, and other settings you choose in the app

The Wi-Fi password and access code are sent only to your controller, over Bluetooth. They are not sent to the developer.

## Permissions

- **Bluetooth** is used to find and talk to your controller.
- **Location** is requested because Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read your GPS position and does not track where you are.
- **Nearby Wi-Fi** is used so you can pick the network the controller should join. The scan stays on the phone.
- **Notifications** are used for inflation status while the app is connected to the controller.

## Android Auto

Android Auto uses the same connection and the same information already stored on the phone. It does not add a separate collection of personal information.

## Sharing

The app does not sell personal information and does not send it to an advertising or analytics service. Information leaves the phone only when the app sends a command or settings to your controller.
